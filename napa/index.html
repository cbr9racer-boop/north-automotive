AUTOCARECENTER

Know the benefits.

North Automotive is a NAPA AutoCare Center. That means ASE-certified technicians, NAPA-quality parts when the job calls for them, and a nationwide warranty on qualifying work — not a handshake and a hope.

There are real benefits to choosing a NAPA AutoCare Center. Our certified ASE technicians make sure you get work you can rely on. The NAPA Peace of Mind® Warranty covers qualifying repairs for 24 months / 24,000 miles. Pay with a NAPA Credit Card and that coverage can extend to 36 months / 36,000 miles. Come in and get the kind of automotive care that still stands behind the invoice.

  • ASE-certified technicians

    Every NAPA AutoCare Center employs ASE-certified techs.

    The National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE) is an independent, non-profit organization that tests and certifies individual automotive repair technicians based on skills, knowledge, and experience. All NAPA AutoCare Centers employ ASE-certified technicians — including the people working on your car here in Thomaston.

  • Peace of Mind Warranty

    24 months / 24,000 miles on qualifying repairs — included free.

    The NAPA AutoCare Peace of Mind® Warranty is included free on qualifying repair or service work. It covers parts and labor for 24 months / 24,000 miles, so you’re protected after you leave the bay — not just until you hit the lot.

  • Honored nationwide

    Drive coast to coast. Coverage follows the car.

    The warranty is honored at more than 14,000 NAPA AutoCare Centers nationwide. Within 25 miles of this shop, call us. Farther than that, contact the NAPA AutoCare Warranty Administrator at 800-452-NAPA (6272).

  • Quality service, every visit

    Automotive service and customer service — both.

    When you choose a NAPA AutoCare Center, you get quality service each and every time: the repair done right, and a person who will still pick up the phone after the invoice. That’s the standard we work to here.

  • NAPA Credit Card

    Ask us about financing — and extra warranty coverage.

    Use a NAPA Credit Card to finance qualifying automotive services and purchases. Pay with it on qualifying repairs and the Peace of Mind coverage can extend to 36 months / 36,000 miles. Not a cardholder? Ask at the counter. Subject to credit approval; terms come from the card issuer.
