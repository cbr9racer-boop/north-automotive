The work

What we actually do

All services

  • Axle, CV joint & driveshaft

    Clicks on a turn, vibration under load, or a torn boot — we find which end actually failed.

  • Belts & hoses

    Serpentine, timing, radiator, heater — the rubber that keeps the rest of the car alive.

  • Brake repair

    Pads, rotors, fluid — measured, not guessed.

  • Cooling system

    Overheating, pink puddles, heater that blows cold. We pressure-test before we sell a radiator.

  • Electrical & electronics

    Warning lights, dead locks, lights that don’t. We trace the circuit instead of throwing parts.

  • Engine diagnostics & performance

    Scan, test, and a plain-English write-up of what failed — and what didn’t.

  • Exhaust system

    Rattle, smell, or a failed cat. We inspect the whole path, not just the pipe you can see.

  • Four-wheel drive

    Transfer case, front axle, and the parts that only matter when the snow hits Thomaston.

  • Preventive maintenance

    Oil, filters, fluids, and a look around so a $80 visit doesn’t become a $1,800 one.

  • Starting, charging & batteries

    Slow crank, battery light, or a car that dies at a light. We load-test before we sell a battery.

  • Steering & suspension

    Wander, clunks, or a wheel that shakes. We check joints, shocks, and the rack.

  • Tire pressure monitoring (TPMS)

    A light that won’t clear, or a sensor that failed with the last set of tires.

  • Tire services

    Mount, balance, rotate, patch — and a straight answer on what you actually need.

  • Transmission service

    Fluid, filter, and a diagnosis before anyone talks rebuild.
