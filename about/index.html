About the shop

Independent repair. NAPA AutoCare.

AUTOCARECENTER

North Automotive LLC, Thomaston.

We’re a local NAPA AutoCare Center at 235 E Main Street — not a chain, not a call center, not a checklist mill. You talk to the people working on the car.

The formula is simple: diagnose it, explain it, fix what’s actually broken. If it can wait, we say so. If we find extra work, we stop and call before we touch it.

ASE-certified technicians. NAPA-quality parts when the job calls for them. And the NAPA Peace of Mind® Warranty on qualifying repairs — 24 months / 24,000 miles, honored at more than 14,000 AutoCare Centers nationwide.

How we work

  • Find it first

    Scan, test, and a write-up in plain English. We don’t throw parts at a guess.

  • Show you the number

    You get the estimate before we start. If something extra turns up, we call.

  • Fix what’s broken

    Then we road-test it. Warranty on the parts and labor we install.
