Services

Auto repair, from belts to brakes.

North Automotive is a full-service NAPA AutoCare Center. Passenger cars, trucks, minivans, and SUVs — we diagnose it, explain it, and fix what’s actually broken. Qualifying work is backed by the Peace of Mind Warranty.

Axle, CV joint & driveshaft

Axles, CV joints, and driveshafts are how power gets to the wheels. A torn boot today is a click tomorrow and a seized joint after that. We inspect both ends, tell you what’s worn, and replace what’s done — not the whole undercarriage for a $12 clamp.

Belts & hoses

Belts and hoses run cooling, charging, A/C, and the engine itself. We check cracks, glaze, leaks, and tension before they leave you on East Main. If a hose is still supple, we say so.

  • Heater hose replacement
  • Radiator hose replacement
  • Serpentine belt replacement
  • Timing belt replacement
Call about this

Brake repair

A working brake system is the one thing we won’t wave off. We measure pad thickness and rotor runout before quoting. If they still have life, we send you home. Hardware, fluid, and a road test are part of the job.

Call about this

Cooling system

Fan, water pump, thermostat, radiator, heater core, hoses. We find the leak or the stuck thermostat first, then replace the part that failed. Coolant service when it’s due — not because a light on the wall said so.

Call about this

Electrical & electronics

Power and data have to actually get where they’re going. Dashboard lights, lighting, power locks, and the rest of the wiring — we diagnose it, then fix the fault. No mystery modules if a ground strap was the whole story.

  • Dashboard warning diagnostic
  • Headlight bulb replacement
  • Interior & exterior lighting
  • Power locks
Call about this

Engine diagnostics & performance

Every car since 1996 has an onboard diagnostics system. Check-engine light, no-start, misfire, or a car that just doesn’t feel right. We scan, test, and write it down in language you can use. Diagnostic time is credited toward the repair if you stay with us.

Call about this

Exhaust system

The exhaust collects and directs emissions out and away. We look at the converter, mid-pipe, muffler, hangers, and flanges. If a clamp will hold, we don’t sell a system.

  • Catalytic converter replacement
  • Center exhaust section
  • Downpipe replacement
  • Exhaust inspection
Call about this

Four-wheel drive

4WD puts power to all four wheels through a transfer case and extra driveline. Binding, noise, or a 4WD light that won’t go out — we inspect the case, axles, and actuators and tell you what’s actually worn.

Call about this

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled service is how you avoid the big invoice. Oil, filters, fluids, and a written inspection of what can wait and what shouldn’t. Conventional, synthetic, and European-spec oils. No $19.99 bait-and-switch.

Call about this

Starting, charging & batteries

Starter, alternator, battery, and the cables between them. We test the system so you don’t replace a battery when the alternator is the problem — or the other way around.

Call about this

Steering & suspension

Steering and suspension keep the car planted and the ride honest. Ball joints, control arms, shocks, struts, tie rods. We show you the play instead of pointing at a bushing that still has years left.

  • Ball joint replacement
Call about this

Tire pressure monitoring (TPMS)

Under-inflation is a safety problem, not a dashboard annoyance. We diagnose the sensor, relearn the module, and replace what’s actually dead — not all four because one beeped.

  • TPMS repair
Call about this

Tire services

Tires are the only part of the car that touches the road. We mount and balance, check TPMS, rotate, and tell you if a patch is enough. No pressure to buy a set of four when two will do the season.

Call about this

Transmission service

The transmission is the link between the engine and the wheels — automatic, manual, or in between. We start with fluid condition and a road test. Service when it’s due. Bigger work only after we can tell you what’s actually wrong.
